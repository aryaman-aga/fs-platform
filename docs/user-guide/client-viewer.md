# Client – Viewer (CFO / director)

You see released drafts and finals of your own company and the management-representation items addressed to you.

1. Sign in with your password and authenticator code.
2. **Released drafts / finals:** view the PDFs (watermarked with your name and the time). Share links you receive
   from the firm expire and work once.
3. **Management confirmations:** read each item (e.g. going-concern assumptions, related-party completeness) and
   confirm or comment. Your confirmation is recorded with a timestamp in the engagement's audit trail.
