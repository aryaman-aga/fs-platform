# Client – Uploader (client's accountant)

You see **only your own company's** engagements and only the screens your role allows.

1. Sign in with the invitation link (valid 72 hours, single use), set your password and scan the QR code with
   an authenticator app. Keep the recovery codes safe — they are shown once.
2. **Upload requests** list what the firm needs (trial balance, workbook, supporting files). Upload `.xlsx`,
   `.xlsm`, `.csv` or `.pdf` files; macros are never run.
3. **Queries** from the engagement team appear in your portal; answer them there, not by e-mail.
4. **Released drafts** (if shared with you) open as watermarked PDFs; download rights depend on your role setting.

Sessions end after 30 minutes without activity.
