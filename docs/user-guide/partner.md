# Partner

**You can:** everything a Manager can, plus approve balancing adjustments, clear review notes, mark the
engagement FINAL and lock it, and (with a Firm Admin) unlock a locked engagement.

## Partner review
1. **Engagement → Summary:** key figures (PBT, PAT, TCI, EPS, total assets, equity, cash; CY and PY), open
   High findings, review notes, disclosure-checklist status, the last PDF verification and the version history.
2. Raise review notes on findings, cells or PDF pages; clear them when resolved.

## Final and lock (06 §1)
*Mark final* is available only when **all** of these hold: zero High findings; all review notes cleared;
signatory checks passed (DIN, FRN, membership number, date, place); a final PDF rendered and verified with zero
misses; disclosure checklist complete; your approval. Locking writes the files with object lock (WORM).

The final PDF has no watermark and is signed outside the platform with your DSC; upload the signed PDF back as
a locked version and record the UDIN.

## Unlocking
Needs you **and** a Firm Admin, with a reason. It creates a new version line; the locked final stays unchanged.
