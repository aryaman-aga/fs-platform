# ADR-0015: Frameworks in year 1: Ind AS Division II standalone and SPFS; consolidation and other frameworks T2

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Deciders:** lead architect (autonomous build); to be ratified by the partners
- **Requirements:** E-PREP-03, E-PREP-04, E-RULE-01, Q16, Q17

## Context

Q16 (consolidation in year 1?) and Q17 (Division I/III, IFRS, US GAAP).

## Decision

Year 1 covers **Schedule III Division II (Ind AS) standalone** statements and **SPFS / interim** layouts for foreign subsidiaries (quarter and year, FC + INR). **Consolidation (E-PREP-03)** and **Division I / III, IFRS and US GAAP template packs (E-PREP-04)** are T2. Rules carry framework applicability in the rule registry so later packs do not change existing behaviour.

## Consequences

- Good: Matches the reviewed engagements; keeps year-1 scope achievable.
- Bad / cost: Group consolidations stay manual in year 1.
