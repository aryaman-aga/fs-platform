# ADR-0014: Assurance target: ISO 27001-aligned controls with SOC 2-ready evidence

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Deciders:** lead architect (autonomous build); to be ratified by the partners
- **Requirements:** 12 §5, E-ITGC-06, Q11

## Context

Q11 (certification or alignment). Certification costs time and money; client due-diligence questionnaires and peer review need evidence now.

## Decision

Operate **ISO/IEC 27001:2022-aligned** controls and produce **SOC 2-ready evidence** (control matrix, quarterly evidence pack, drills, access reviews, change log) without committing to certification in year 1. Revisit after one full year of evidence.

## Consequences

- Good: Evidence exists from day one; certification later is incremental.
- Bad / cost: No certificate to show clients in year 1.
